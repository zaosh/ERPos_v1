OVERVIEW

Four connected flows.

Every operation in the store is covered — from the moment a new item walks in the door to the moment it sells, gets returned, or gets swapped.

📸

Intake — photo in, barcode out

Staff snaps a photo of the item. AI reads the image and pre-fills the colour, type, and style. Staff reviews, sets a price, clicks confirm. A barcode label prints automatically at the counter. No typing, no handwriting.

↓ 8 seconds per item
🛍

Checkout — scan once, sale done

Cashier scans barcodes with a handheld scanner. Each item shows its name, colour, and condition instantly. Link a customer by phone number, apply a discount, press F12 — receipt prints with a full breakdown. Every sale is recorded permanently.

↓ under 20 seconds per transaction
↕️

Exchange — swap without the guesswork

Customer wants to swap something they bought. Staff looks them up by phone — their purchase history loads. The system shows the original intake photo of the item so staff can visually confirm it's the same piece. Pick a replacement, confirm, done. The full event is logged on the bill.

↓ under 1 minute
📊

Analytics — real numbers, real time

The dashboard shows today's revenue, what sold, what's sitting unsold, and which labels move fastest. No counting the till, no end-of-week guesswork. Everything updates as sales happen.

↓ updated live

Exchange

Swap with
confidence.

Customer comes back wanting to swap something. Staff types their phone number — eligible items appear from their purchase history. Exchange sales tracked separately — doesn't skew your analytics

CAPABILITIES

Everything in one place.

Every part of the store's operations — covered by a single system running on a computer at the counter.

🤖
AI item analysis
Reads the intake photo and auto-fills colour, type, and garment style. Staff always reviews before saving — the AI is a suggestion, not a decision.
🏷️
Automatic barcode labels
Every confirmed item gets a unique barcode printed on a thermal label instantly. Works with any standard Zebra-compatible label printer.
📦
Bulk intake
Got 20 identical plain tees? One confirmation, 20 individual barcodes. Each item tracked separately but entered in one step.
🛍
Point of sale
Full POS with barcode scanning, live cart, price override per item, discount (flat or percentage), customer linking, and receipt printing.
👤
Customer records
Phone number lookup builds a purchase history automatically over time. Know who's returned, what they've bought, and how many times.
↩️
Returns
Returns validated against the original sale record — the system checks the item was on the bill and is within the return window. No more "trust the customer".
↕️
Exchanges
Full exchange flow with photo identity confirmation, exchange window enforcement, bill history timeline, and separate tracking from regular sales data.
📊
Real-time analytics
Revenue, sell-through, dead stock, and velocity metrics — all live. Filter by 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days. No exports needed.
🧾
Bill history
Every bill has a full event timeline: when it was sold, which items were exchanged, which were returned, and by whom. One look, full story.
🔒
Role-based access
Staff: intake and checkout only. Admin: analytics, voids, reports, exchanges. Superadmin: system settings. Each role sees exactly what they need.
🖼️
Image archive
Every item's intake photo is stored forever — even after it sells. Original photos are the identity record for the item. Never deleted, never lost.
⚙️
Configurable settings
Return window, exchange window, exchange fee, tax rate, store name — all adjustable by admin without touching any code.
